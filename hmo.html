Hückel MO Calculator

Enter a SMILES string or draw the molecule. The calculator symmetrizes the structure, finds the π system, builds and diagonalizes the Hückel matrix, and labels every orbital by symmetry. Energies are given as E = α + xβ with β < 0.

Reads the organic subset, aromatic lowercase atoms, bracket atoms such as [CH-], [nH], [N+], [CH2] (radical), ring closures and branches. Hydrogens are implicit.

Click a bond with a ring tool to fuse a ring. Click an atom with a ring or group to attach it. With the bond tool, click a bond to change its order (single → double → triple → aromatic) or drag from an atom.
