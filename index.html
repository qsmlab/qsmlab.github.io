Quantum Simulation of Molecules Lab

We use quantum chemistry, quantum dynamics and quantum computing to understand how electrons, spins and nuclei together decide the behaviour of molecules.

Led by Prof. Sabyashachi Mishra · Department of Chemistry, IIT Kharagpur
